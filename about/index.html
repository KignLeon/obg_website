Our Origin

San Diego's Old School Boxing Gym

Old School Boxing & Fitness Center was built on a belief that boxing is more than a sport — it's a discipline that sharpens the mind, forges the body, and builds character that lasts a lifetime.

Founded and led by Head Coach Ernest Johnson, our gym has trained everyone from first-timers to competitive fighters. We don't believe in shortcuts. We believe in hard work, proper technique, and showing up every single day.

Located in the heart of San Diego at 5987 El Cajon Blvd, we welcome students of all ages and skill levels — from kids learning their first jab to adults looking to get in the best shape of their lives.

What We Stand For

The Old School Way

Discipline

Every champion was built in the gym before the arena. We teach consistency, focus, and the habits that create lasting transformation.

Grit

We don't believe in easy. We believe in earning it. Every round, every rep, every drop of sweat is an investment in who you're becoming.

Community

Inside these walls, everyone belongs. From beginners to competitors, we train together, push each other, and celebrate every win.

Technique

Old School doesn't mean outdated. It means fundamentals done right. Proper stance, footwork, and combinations are the foundation of everything.

Youth First

Our youth program is one of the best in San Diego. We build confidence, discipline, and character in young athletes that extends far beyond the gym.

Excellence

We hold our students and ourselves to the highest standard — not to be perfect, but to continuously improve and never stop growing.
